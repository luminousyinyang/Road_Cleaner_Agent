Simulation

Rare events are the hard part.

An autonomous stack sees a million miles of ordinary road and almost no shed truck tyres. Road Cleaner already watches public cameras across three states for exactly those events — so once it has found one, it can re-stage it from a dashcam view and hand back the footage that was missing.

Everything on this page is generated. The detections are real: found on public traffic cameras, gated, and reported to the agency that owns the road. The clips are not. They are re-stagings produced by {{ veo_model }}, seeded by the detection record rather than by the camera image, and they never form part of the evidence attached to a filed report.

Hazards involving a person are not simulated at all — the same “roads, not people” rule that governs the rest of the system.
