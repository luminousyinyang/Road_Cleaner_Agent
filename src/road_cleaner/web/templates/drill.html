Watch the fleet work.

The road log shows hazards the fleet has already found. This runs it in front of you instead. Describe a hazard, drop a pin anywhere in the lower 48, and the same four agents that read a traffic camera will stage it, look twice, decide whether it is worth reporting, work out whose road it is, and write the report.

Then it stops. A drill case is marked synthetic, kept out of the statistics, invisible to the Auditor, and the Dispatcher raises rather than files if anything tries to send one. The Send button is there, and it cannot be pressed.
