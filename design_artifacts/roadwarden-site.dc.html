So I do.

There's a truck tire in lane two of I-285 right now. You can't stop. The camera pointed at it isn't being watched. And even if you wanted to report it, good luck guessing whether that half-mile belongs to the state, the county, or a toll authority.

RoadWarden is a small crew of software agents that watches public DOT cameras around the clock, spots the junk, figures out whose road it is, files the report, and keeps checking back until it's gone.
