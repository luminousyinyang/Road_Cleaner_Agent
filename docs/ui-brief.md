# Road Cleaner — UI brief for the simulation pivot

**For:** whoever rebuilds the dashboard.
**Status of the backend:** done and working. This is a front-end and narrative job.
**Deadline:** hackathon submission 2026-08-31.

---

## 1. What changed

Road Cleaner started as a civic tool: watch public traffic cameras across GA/FL/NC,
find hazards the official 511 feeds missed, work out whose road it is, file the
report, keep checking until it is fixed. That all still runs, unchanged.

What is new is what happens *after* a hazard is confirmed. An autonomous driving
stack sees a million miles of ordinary road and almost no shed truck tyres —
rare events are exactly what it lacks and exactly what a camera fleet sees all
day. So a confirmed hazard is now also a **scenario**: Veo re-stages it as
dashcam footage, from the perspective a perception stack actually trains on.

**The pitch, one sentence:**

> Road Cleaner mines live public traffic cameras for real road-hazard edge cases,
> reports each one to the agency that owns the road, and turns it into synthetic
> dashcam footage for training autonomous vehicles.

Detection is the engine. The civic reporting is proof the detections are real and
consequential. The synthetic footage is the product.

## 2. The gap to close

The site does not tell this story yet.

| Page | Says today | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| `/` road log | *"It finds the hazard, then files the paperwork."* | The old thesis. No hint the project produces training data. |
| `/about` | *"Detection is the easy part"* → 7-stage civic pipeline | Entirely the old framing. |
| `/simulation` | The new story | Bolted on, third in the nav, reachable only if you go looking. |
| `/cases/{id}` | Evidence, trail, letter, then generated clips at the bottom | Simulation reads as an afterthought. |

**The core ask:** restructure so a judge landing on `/` understands the AV
simulation thesis within about five seconds, without the civic reporting work
being demoted to a footnote — it is what makes the data real.

## 3. Suggested information architecture

Not prescriptive; the goal matters more than the shape.

- **`/` — the new front door.** Lead with the AV thesis. The strongest single
  visual in the project is the **side-by-side pair**: fixed pole-mounted camera
  frame on the left, photorealistic generated dashcam clip on the right, captioned
  *"what the camera saw"* / *"what a car would have seen"*. That pair currently
  lives buried on `/simulation`. It should be the hero.
- **A scenario library.** The generated clips as a browsable set — hazard type,
  road type, state, weather/lighting. This is the "product" view: what you'd get
  if you were shopping for edge-case training data.
- **The road log.** Keep it, largely as-is. It is the evidence that the scenarios
  come from real events, and its case pages are genuinely good.
- **How it works.** Rewrite to cover both halves: detect → gate → report →
  simulate. The existing 7-stage explanation is accurate and worth keeping as the
  first half.

Five clips exist today (debris, flooding, stalled vehicle, animal, damaged
guardrail) plus one spoken briefing and one music bed. Design for ~10s of video
per case, 16:9, 1920×1080.

## 4. Hard constraints — do not negotiate these

The project's credibility rests on its reports being checkable. Generated media
must never be mistakable for camera evidence.

1. **Every generated clip carries a visible badge** naming the model that made it
   (`SYNTHETIC — generated by veo-3.1-fast-generate-001`). The badge text comes
   from the clip's `.json` provenance sidecar. It is not decoration and must not
   be removed, softened, made hover-only, or rendered as a subtle watermark.
2. **Generated media and camera evidence stay visually distinct.** Different
   containers, different treatment. Never interleave them in one gallery where a
   viewer could mistake one for the other.
3. **Never imply a generated clip is camera footage** in any copy. "Re-staged",
   "synthesised", "simulated" — not "footage of the incident".
4. **Nothing generated may appear in the filed-report view.** The letter, the
   evidence filmstrip, and the audit trail are the civic record. Tests enforce
   this server-side; do not route around it.
5. **Hazards involving a person are never simulated.** `pedestrian_on_highway`
   is refused by name. If a case page shows one, the generate control must show
   the refusal reason, not a disabled button with no explanation.

## 5. Technical constraints

- **Keep server-rendered Jinja2 with no build step.** The whole app ships as one
  Cloud Run image (`Dockerfile`) with no Node toolchain. A React/Vite
  rewrite breaks the deployment story for zero judged benefit. Plain CSS and
  vanilla JS only.
- **Reuse `web/static/css/tokens.css`.** It is a port of the design comps and
  carries the palette and type scale. The site deliberately runs two registers —
  a warm "field notebook" for the log (Instrument Serif + JetBrains Mono on
  off-white, hard offset shadows) and a heavier editorial voice for the explainer
  (Overpass + Zilla Slab, forest green). That contrast is intentional. A third
  register for the simulation surface would be reasonable — something colder and
  more technical, to read as tooling rather than civic paperwork.
- Existing templates: `base.html`, `log.html`, `case.html`, `about.html`,
  `simulation.html`, `not_found.html`, `partials/hero_art.html`.
- Existing JS: `case.js` (recheck + countdown), `simulate.js` (render job polling).

## 6. Data available

All of this exists and works. No backend changes needed for the pages above.

### Pages
`GET /` · `GET /cases/{case_id}` · `GET /simulation` · `GET /about`

### JSON
| Endpoint | Returns |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/stats` | `filed_this_week`, `missed_by_feed_pct`, `median_detect_to_file_seconds`, `total_cases`, `open_cases`, `count_{cleared,escalated,filed,suppressed,watching}` |
| `GET /api/cases` | `{cases: [...]}` — each: `id, kind, status, tone, hazard, location, when, sentence, ref_label, ref, frame_url, box_style, box_label, box_clear, suppressed` |
| `GET /api/cases/{id}` | `case, state_code, number, status, tone, watching, camera_label, road, county, live_frame, box_style, box_label, frames, trail, agency, channel, reference, ref_label, sla, letter, letter_subject, was_dry_run, filing_count, last_checked, model_json, model_name, explain` |
| `GET /api/cameras` | camera registry |
| `POST /api/cases/{id}/recheck` | runs the real Auditor |

### Media
- `GET /frames/{blob_key}` — **camera evidence**, JPEG.
- `GET /media/{blob_key}` — **generated media**, with HTTP Range so video seeks.
  Refuses anything not under `synthetic/`. Keys look like
  `synthetic/GA-4462/2026-08-22T10-44-01-veo-3.1-fast-generate-001.mp4`.
- Each clip has a sibling `.json` sidecar: `model_name`, `prompt`,
  `seeded_from_case_id`, `duration_seconds`, `size_bytes`, `created_at`.

### Generation
- `POST /api/simulate/{case_id}` → `202` + job object. `409` if
  `MEDIA_PROVIDER != vertex`; `404` unknown case; `422` hazard we refuse to
  simulate (message explains why).
- `GET /api/simulate/jobs/{job_id}` → `{state: running|done|failed, elapsed,
  estimated_fraction, typical_seconds, clip_url, clip_badge, error}`.
- A render takes ~2 minutes at 1080p and **bills per second of video**. The
  control must state the cost. One render per case at a time (enforced server-side).
- **The progress bar is an estimate and must say so.** Veo reports no percentage —
  a job is running or done. The current bar is elapsed time against a typical
  render, capped below full while running. Do not replace it with an animation
  that implies real progress.

### One gap you may need to fill
There is no JSON endpoint listing a case's generated media — `media_for_case()`
in `web/serializers.py` reads the filesystem server-side. If the new UI wants to
fetch clips client-side (e.g. a lazy-loaded scenario library), add a small
`GET /api/media/{case_id}` alongside it.

## 7. Copy notes

- The tone is dry, concrete, slightly wry. It never oversells. Keep that.
- Be honest about what is simulated. The current `/simulation` page opens with
  *"Everything on this page is generated"* and explains the boundary. Keep that
  paragraph or an equivalent — it reads as confidence, not as a disclaimer.
- Real numbers only. Every stat on the site is computed from the database. Do not
  add invented metrics.
- Known artifact worth not drawing attention to: Veo sometimes stamps a garbled
  timestamp into a corner of a clip. Do not crop it in a way that hides the badge.

## 8. Out of scope

Do not change: `domain/`, `ports/`, `adapters/`, `agents/`, `pipeline/`,
`container.py`, `config.py`, or anything under `tests/` that asserts the
evidence/synthetic boundary. The backend is finished and covered by 264 tests.
